The Three Months

One question per month, answered properly

See the child, understand the child, then set up the environment the child can actually work in.

01
Month one

Decoding the Child's Perspective

Before any method, material or correction, there is one skill: seeing the situation the way the child sees it. The first month is spent learning to look.

02
Month two

Early Childhood Psychology

What is actually happening inside a child in the first six years, from attachment and temperament to language, movement and learning to self-regulate.

03
Month three

The Montessori Curriculum in Practice

The hands-on month, starting with practical life, the most underestimated shelf in any Montessori classroom and the easiest to recreate at home. You present exercises yourself.
