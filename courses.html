The Three Months

One question per month, answered properly

Each month stands on the one before it: see the child, understand the child, then set up the environment the child can actually work in.

01 Month One

Decoding the Child's Perspective

Before any method, any material and any correction, there is one skill: seeing the situation the way the child is seeing it. The first month is spent learning to look.

02 Month Two

Early Childhood Psychology

What is actually happening inside a child in the first six years, from attachment and temperament to language, movement and the long work of learning to self-regulate.

03 Month Three

The Montessori Curriculum in Practice

The hands-on month, starting with practical life, the most underestimated shelf in any Montessori classroom and the easiest to recreate at home. You will present exercises, not just watch them.

Good For

Who this course is for

Teachers

Montessori directresses, playgroup and daycare staff, and anyone running a room full of three-year-olds with more instinct than training.

Caregivers

Grandparents, nannies and anyone else who spends whole days with a small child and wants to do it with more understanding.

Parents

Especially of under-sixes, and especially if you would rather understand the behaviour than manage it.

Education & psychology students

Developmental theory with the practical half attached, and a certificate to show for three months of work.
