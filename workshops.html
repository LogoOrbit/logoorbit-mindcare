The Three Days

One question per day, answered properly

Each day stands on the one before it: see the child, understand the child, then set up the environment the child can actually work in.

01 Day One

Decoding the Child's Perspective

Before any method, any material and any correction, there is one skill: seeing the situation the way the child is seeing it. We spend the first day learning to look.

02 Day Two

Early Childhood Psychology

What is actually happening inside a child in the first six years, from attachment and temperament to language, movement and the long work of learning to self-regulate.

03 Day Three

The Practical Life Area of the Montessori Curriculum

The most underestimated shelf in any Montessori classroom, and the easiest to recreate at home. A hands-on day: you will present exercises, not just watch them.
