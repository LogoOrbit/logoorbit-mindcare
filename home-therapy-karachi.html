A psychologist who comes home

Sometimes the hardest part of getting help is getting out of the door. Traffic, a child who melts down in waiting rooms, a parent who can no longer travel, or simply not wanting anyone to see you walk into a clinic. So MindCare Services® now brings therapy to you.

Whether you searched for home therapy, a home psychologist, a therapist home visit or in-home counseling in Karachi, this is it: a qualified MindCare psychologist or therapist, at your home, at a time that suits your family.

Why families choose home sessions

  • No traffic, no travel, no waiting room
  • Ideal for children, elderly parents and anyone who finds clinics hard
  • Discreet visits: nobody needs to know who is coming or why
  • Therapy built into real life, where change has to stick
  • Qualified psychologists and therapists, English or Urdu
  • Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm, across Karachi

  • Child psychologist

Who It's For

Home sessions for every member of the family

Adults

Anxiety, depression, stress, panic, OCD, grief or burnout, without the commute or the waiting room.

Children

Behavior, ADHD, autism support, speech and learning concerns, worked on where your child plays and learns.

Teenagers

Exam pressure, low mood, anger or withdrawal, with a therapist who meets them on their own ground.

Couples & families

Conflict, distance or a hard life change, worked through together at your own table.

Elderly parents

Loneliness, low mood, memory worries or loss, for parents who can't easily travel.

Special needs

Structured remedial, occupational and behavioral therapy built into daily home routines.

How It Works

From message to first home session

1. Message or call

Tell us who the session is for, what's going on and your area. WhatsApp is quickest.

2. We match a specialist

We pair you with the right psychologist or therapist and agree a time that suits your home.

3. We come to you

Your therapist arrives on time, discreetly, with no signage, no uniform and no fuss.

4. Keep going your way

Continue at home, switch to clinic or online, or mix all three as life changes.
